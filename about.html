Founder & Host

Meet Pastor Kay
(Kaylette Blunt)

Kaylette Blunt — known to the ministry as Pastor Kay — is an assistant pastor who has preached the gospel for over 30 years, carrying a powerful prophetic anointing. She serves as a Pastor, Prophetic Voice, and Exhorter. She currently serves as Assistant Pastor at Life By Design Ministries in Aurora, Colorado. In 2021, she launched Encouragement Fuel for the Journey as a weekly audio podcast — a Word-based ministry built on biblical truth, prayer, and practical teaching.

Our Mission

Encouragement Fuel for the Journey exists to encourage, strengthen, and equip believers through the Word of God and practical teaching—to help you grow in faith, walk through the hard places, heal, mature, and keep moving forward in the purpose God has for your life.

Our Vision

To see believers become rooted in the Word of God, strengthened for their journey, and equipped to fulfill their God-given destiny.
