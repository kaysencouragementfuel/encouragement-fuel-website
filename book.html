The Book · By Pastor Kay

The Sound of Forward Motion

Moving forward when staying still is no longer an option.

There comes a time when staying where you are is no longer comfortable—it becomes costly.

The Sound of Forward Motion is for the person who knows God is calling them forward, but fear, disappointment, delay, or uncertainty has made it difficult to move. This book will challenge you to hear God clearly, trust Him deeply, and respond with obedience, courage, and faith.

Your next season will require movement. Purchase your copy today and begin moving toward everything God has called you to become.
